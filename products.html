LLC “Low Loss Concept” Transformers - Energy Efficient and highly redundant power distribution system

The Low Loss Concept (LLC) is a power distribution system comprising higher efficiency, lower weight and volume, and a high system redundancy, represents the cutting edge of HV – LV Dry Type Transformer technology. The basic motive for the design is to reduce and eliminate the need for supply (pulse) transformers to the frequency converters, especially those supplying electric propulsion.

LLC Transformers are available for both Low Voltage and Medium Voltage applications. It is effective also in vessels with operating profiles that require variable speeds, such as OSVs. The LLC power distribution system has been patented internationally by the Wärtsilä Group, ISOTECH its supplier’ partner for LLC Transformers since 2004.
