OUR MARKETS

At ISOTECH, we don't just provide components; we offer comprehensive strategic support throughout the entire lifecycle of your energy projects. We stand as the partner of choice for global players seeking technical excellence, deep industry knowledge, and unwavering commercial reliability.

APPLICATION

Marine & Offshore

We provide expert support for the design and supply of transformers and electrical equipment engineered to withstand harsh marine environments, ensuring operational continuity and safety on vessels and offshore platforms.

Oil & Gas

We operate in high-risk environments where technical precision is paramount. We offer specific solutions for extraction and refining infrastructures, where the robustness of electrical components is an essential requirement.

Industrial: Metal, Steel & Mining

We support heavy industry — including steel mills, mining plants, and production sites — by providing specialized consulting on reactors and electrical systems capable of handling high power loads and intensive production cycles.

Renewables: Solar, Wind & Water Treatment

We are active players in the energy transition. We support solar and wind energy projects, as well as the water treatment sector, with optimized components designed to maximize the efficiency of renewable sources.

Utilities & EPC Companies

We work closely with Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) companies and major public utility providers, offering a strategic bridge between complex engineering design and on-site implementation.

Power Generation, Transmission & Distribution

From power plants to end-user distribution. Our expertise covers the entire power supply chain, ensuring that energy is generated, transmitted, and distributed according to the highest technical efficiency standards.
