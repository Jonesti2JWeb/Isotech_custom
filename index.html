WHO WE ARE

ISOTECH is an Italian Technical Trading, Marketing & Sales consulting company with over 30 years of uninterrupted success. We boast long-standing experience in the industrial energy field, with a specific focus on On-Load and Off-Load Tap Changers, both oil and vacuum types, Transformers, Reactors, and electrical equipment. Our expertise addresses the needs of the industrial sector, power utility companies, and the Marine-Offshore industry.

SINCE 1989

HISTORY

Established in 1989 by Antonio Nigro, ISOTECH has grown from an Italian consulting firm into an international strategic partner. For three decades, we have focused on delivering high-performance electrical equipment, bridging the gap between complex engineering needs and cutting-edge production.

OUR VISION & CORE VALUES

OUR MISSION

“Connecting technological innovation to energy challenges.”

Our mission is to guide industry leaders across the Marine & Offshore sectors toward energy efficiency and operational safety by providing cutting-edge electromechanical solutions and transferring technical know-how to optimize complex projects.

STRATEGIC ADVANTAGE

WHY CHOOSE US

Choosing ISOTECH means partnering with a long-standing specialist in the energy, marine, and electromechanical sectors, dedicated to delivering tailor-made solutions with exceptional flexibility and rapid turnaround times. We bridge the gap between clients and top-tier manufacturers, leveraging a proven international track record with industry leaders like Wärtsilä to ensure every project meets the highest technical standards.
